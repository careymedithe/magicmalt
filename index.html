Frequently asked

Everything you need to begin your Milletarian journey.

What makes Magic Malt™ special?

It combines finger millet and soluble fibre in an instant drink that needs no cooking. It is available in Traditional, Chocolate and Savoury flavours.

How do I prepare it?

Add one sachet to approximately 150 ml of hot water, stir well until smooth and enjoy. Adjust the water to suit your preferred consistency.

Is it safe and certified?

The source site states that Magic Malt™ is FSSAI certified, dairy-free, soy-free and made without preservatives or artificial flavours.

Does it contain gluten?

Finger millet is naturally gluten-free. Customers with severe allergies or coeliac disease should confirm manufacturing and cross-contamination information directly with the brand before use.
